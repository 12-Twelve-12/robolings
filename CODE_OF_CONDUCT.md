# Code of conduct

This is a repository people come to in order to learn something they do not
know yet. That sets the tone: questions are the point, and not knowing
something is never a reason to be talked down to.

## What is expected

- Assume the person asking has a reason to ask. "Read the docstring" is not an
  answer; pointing at the line of the docstring that settles it is.
- Review the code, not the person.
- If you disagree with a design decision, say what it costs and what you would
  do instead. "This is wrong" on its own is not reviewable.
- English or Chinese are both fine, in issues, in pull requests, and in the
  exercises you contribute.

## What is not

Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks. Sexualised language or
imagery. Publishing anyone's private information. Sustained disruption of a
discussion.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to the maintainer, [@12-Twelve-12](https://github.com/12-Twelve-12),
through a GitHub issue if it can be discussed in the open, or through the
contact address on that profile if it cannot. Reports are read, and the person
who reported is not identified without their agreement.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull
requests, and block accounts. Where that happens for conduct reasons rather
than technical ones, it will be said so.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
